# Utylity Mascot

Uty and Lity are Mod Companion's mascot created by [Tyson Tan](https://tysontan.com/), dual-licensed under CC-BY-SA and MIT as part of Mod Companion's branding.

---

<img src="/img/utylity.png" style="max-height: 80dvh; margin-inline: auto">

Lity is a game developer with cotton candy hair. She represents the human creativity and artistic spark that flows into video games. 

Uty is a cubic robot on wheels. He represents the game technology and tooling Lity needs to turn her dreams into reality. He can open up like a toolbox, with new technology for Lity to use.

Uty and Lity: Technological Innovation and Human Creativity. Both need each other to make great games.
